# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small community of anime, game, manga, and movie enthusiasts who want a shared or personal way to track their media collection. Users range from casual viewers/players to dedicated fans who want stats, progress tracking, and a clean way to manage what they've watched, played, or plan to. Context: typically on mobile or desktop, quick to add items, review progress, and glance at dashboard stats.

## Product Purpose

My Media Tracker is a Progressive Web App that lets users log, organize, and analyze their personal media collections — Games, Anime, Manga, and Movies. It replaces spreadsheets and cluttering bookmarks with a focused tool: add items (with auto-fill from Jikan API), track progress with ratings and reviews, see real-time stats via charts, and manage data with batch operations, undo/redo, and JSON export/import. Success looks like: the user opens the app, finds what they want to update in seconds, and feels their collection is well-organized.

## Brand Personality

Clean, friendly, modern. Approachable structure with warmth — not corporate, not gamer-dark. Think Notion meets MyAnimeList: organized and capable, but visually welcoming. Thai-first UI with English fallback. Small touches of delight (emoji, micro-animations) without sacrificing clarity.

## Anti-references

- **Generic Bootstrap** — cookie-cutter buttons, default blue, no personality.
- **Cluttered / dense layouts** — avoid information-dense tables like old MyAnimeList or spreadsheet-style UIs. Keep it breathable with clear visual hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every surface should be scannable. Information first, ornament second.
2. **Dark mode is a first-class citizen** — Themes are not an afterthought; dark and light are equally polished.
3. **Mobile-first, not mobile-awkward** — Touch targets, readable text, and usable modals on small screens before desktop refinements.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Show the common case (add item, view list) upfront; hide complexity (batch ops, import/export) until needed.
5. **Respect the data** — Undo/redo, safe deletes, and export/import reflect that users trust the app with their personal collection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No strict WCAG level mandated. Basic good practices apply: readable contrast in both themes, keyboard-accessible inputs and buttons, and semantic HTML where practical. Reduced-motion preference should disable non-essential animations.
